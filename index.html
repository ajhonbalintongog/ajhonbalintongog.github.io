Best Thesis · 2023

Integrated Motorcycle Safety System (MCSS) — IoT Capstone

An IoT safety system that keeps a motorcycle from starting unless the rider is wearing the helmet and smartwatch and is fit to ride — and that texts an emergency contact with the GPS location when a crash is detected. Best Thesis Awardee, batch 2023.

How it works
  1. Setup — the rider pairs the helmet and watch by serial number and sets an emergency contact in the Android app.
  2. Pre-ride check — the helmet confirms it is worn and tests breath alcohol; the watch reads heart rate and SpO2. Both report to the safety box.
  3. Ignition interlock — the engine starts only if alcohol < 0.05 g/dl, heart rate is 60–100 bpm, SpO2 is 95–100%, and helmet and watch are worn.
  4. While riding — continuous monitoring; removing the helmet or watch, or abnormal vitals, activates the kill switch.
  5. Crash response — the MPU6050 detects impact, a buzzer warns by severity, and the SIM800L sends an SMS with the NEO-6M GPS location.
System design
Smart helmet nodeMQ3 breath-alcohol check plus IR and FSR sensors to confirm the helmet is worn; reports over ESP-NOW.
Biometric smartwatchMAX30102 heart rate and SpO2 with an SSD1306 OLED display; reports over ESP-NOW.
Safety box hubControls ignition through a relay and kill switch, detects crashes with an MPU6050, and sends GSM/GPS alerts.
Android companion appLive dashboard (alcohol, heart rate, SpO2, helmet/watch status) and settings: owner, device serials, emergency contact, custom SMS, hotspot.
Embedded web serverC++ firmware runs a Wi-Fi soft AP and HTTP server on the hub, with settings saved in SPIFFS flash storage.
Works fully offlineThe phone connects straight to the safety box hotspot — no cloud or mobile data needed.
Design decisions
  • Locked by default: the engine stays locked and unlocks only when every check passes.
  • ESP-NOW over Wi-Fi routing: direct, low-latency device-to-device links with no router — suited to a moving motorcycle.
  • Validated, not assumed: rated 4.70 for reliability, effectiveness and user-friendliness and 4.67 for accuracy; top items were consistent operation (4.82), crash SMS alert (4.78) and app-sensor connectivity (4.76).
  • Low cost: about PHP 3,372 in hardware, built through an iterative SDLC from design to field testing.
